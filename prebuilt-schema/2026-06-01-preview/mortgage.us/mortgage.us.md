|`mortgage.us.1003`|[`prebuilt-mortgage.us.1003`](mortgage.us.1003.md)|Uniform Residential Loan Application (URLA). Borrower-completed form with sections like Borrower Information, Financial Information—Assets and Liabilities, Real Estate Owned, Declarations, Acknowledgments and Agreements, and Demographic Information (HMDA). Includes property/loan purpose, income, housing expenses, and loan originator details; signed by borrower(s). Often labeled Fannie Mae Form 1003/Freddie Mac Form 65. Distinct from underwriting summaries (1008), appraisal reports (1004), and employer verifications (1005).|
|`mortgage.us.1004`|[`prebuilt-mortgage.us.1004`](mortgage.us.1004.md)|Uniform Residential Appraisal Report (URAR). Appraiser-prepared valuation with sections: Subject, Contract, Neighborhood, Site, Improvements, Sales Comparison Approach with adjustment grid, Cost/Income approaches (as applicable), Comments, Appraiser's Certification and Limiting Conditions. Includes effective date of appraisal, opinion of value, photos (front/rear/street), map, sketch. Often references Fannie Mae 1004/Freddie Mac 70. Not an application (1003) or underwriting summary (1008).|
|`mortgage.us.1005`|[`prebuilt-mortgage.us.1005`](mortgage.us.1005.md)|Verification of Employment (VOE). Employer-completed form confirming borrower's employment status and income. Fields for employer name/address, position/title, start/end dates, base pay, overtime/bonus/commission (YTD and prior years), probability of continued employment; signed by employer/HR. May include a borrower authorization at top. Not an appraisal (1004) or loan application (1003).|
|`mortgage.us.1008`|[`prebuilt-mortgage.us.1008`](mortgage.us.1008.md)|Transmittal Summary (Underwriting summary). Lender/underwriter form summarizing loan and borrower risk: loan purpose, occupancy, property type, LTV/CLTV/HCLTV, DTI ratios, credit score, AUS findings, MI coverage, reserves, cash to close, income calculations, compensating factors, conditions. Often titled Transmittal Summary and referenced as Fannie Mae 1008/Freddie Mac 1077. Distinct from the borrower-signed 1003 and the appraisal 1004.|
|`mortgage.us.closingDisclosure`|[`prebuilt-mortgage.us.closingDisclosure`](mortgage.us.closingDisclosure.md)|TILA-RESPA Closing Disclosure (TRID). Five-page final disclosure with standardized sections: Loan Terms, Projected Payments, Costs at Closing, Closing Cost Details (Loan Costs/Other Costs), Calculating Cash to Close, Summaries of Transactions, Adjustments and Credits, Loan Disclosures, Contact Information, and Confirm Receipt. Shows borrower/seller amounts and cash to close. Distinct from the initial Loan Estimate (which should not be classified here).|
|`other`|`prebuilt-documentFields`|Any document not matching the category above. Use other for partial excerpts or heavily redacted pages where the form type cannot be confidently identified.|
