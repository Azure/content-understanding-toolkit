| Category | Analyzer ID | Description |
|:------|:-----|:--------|
|`receipt.generic`|[`prebuilt-receipt.generic`](receipt.generic.md)|Proof of payment for retail/restaurant or general POS transactions. Often shows “Receipt” or “Sales Receipt,” store name/location, date/time, itemized products or services (SKU/PLU), subtotal, tax, tip (optional), total paid, payment method (cash/card last 4), and change due. Exclude hotel folios (use receipt.hotel).|
|`receipt.hotel`|[`prebuilt-receipt.hotel`](receipt.hotel.md)|Hotel folio or lodging receipt acknowledging payment for a stay. Includes guest name, hotel name/address, room number/type, check-in/out dates, nightly rate, room charges, incidentals (parking, minibar), taxes/fees (occupancy/resort), and total paid/balance. May say “Folio,” “Guest Statement,” or “Hotel Receipt.” Exclude generic store/restaurant receipts.|
|`other`|`prebuilt-documentFields`|Any document not matching the category above. Use other for partial excerpts or heavily redacted pages where the form type cannot be confidently identified.|
