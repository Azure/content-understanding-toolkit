| Category | Analyzer ID | Description |
|:------|:-----|:--------|
|`invoice`|[`prebuilt-invoice`](invoice.md)|Seller-issued billing document requesting payment for goods or services. Typically includes an invoice title, invoice number, invoice date, seller and customer (bill-to/ship-to) identities, itemized charges (description, quantity, unit price), tax/VAT breakdown, subtotal and total amount due/payable, and payment terms (due date, net terms) and/or remittance/bank details.|
|`receipt.generic`|[`prebuilt-receipt.generic`](receipt.generic.md)|Point-of-sale or immediate-payment proof of purchase. Typically includes merchant/store name and location, date and time, register/cashier/terminal or ticket/memo identifiers, item lines (often short product descriptions and prices), subtotal/tax/total, and payment confirmation details such as payment method (cash/card), authorization/reference, amount paid, and change/balance. Often appears as “Sales Receipt” and may also be titled “Tax Invoice”, “Retail Invoice”, or “Bill of Supply” when produced by retail POS systems.|
|`receipt.hotel`|[`prebuilt-receipt.hotel`](receipt.hotel.md)|Lodging folio or hotel stay statement. Typically includes hotel name/address, guest name, room number/type, arrival/check-in and departure/check-out dates, a ledger-style list of charges (nightly room rates, taxes/fees, incidentals such as breakfast/parking/minibar) and payments/credits, and a final balance (often zero when settled). May be labeled as folio, guest statement, or hotel invoice/receipt.|
|`purchaseOrder`|[`prebuilt-purchaseOrder`](purchaseOrder.md)|Buyer-issued ordering document authorizing a vendor to provide goods or services. Typically labeled as purchase order/order/ordre/ordine or order acknowledgement and includes a PO/order number, order date, buyer and supplier details, ship-to/deliver-to location, requested delivery/required-by date, and ordered line items (part numbers/descriptions, quantities, unit costs). Often includes approval/authorization or project/job/lot references.|
|`utilityBill`|[`prebuilt-utilityBill`](utilityBill.md)|Recurring service bill/statement for ongoing utilities and similar metered or subscription services such as electricity, gas, water/sewer, waste services, and telecom/internet. Typically includes an account number, service address or service identifier (meter number, phone number, circuit/line ID), billing period/service dates, prior balance plus payments/credits/adjustments, current charges summary, usage or activity details (e.g., kWh/therms/gallons/GB/minutes or plan charges), total amount due and due date, and often a remittance slip or payment options section.|
|`creditMemo`|[`prebuilt-creditMemo`](creditMemo.md)|Billing adjustment document that changes the amount previously billed. Typically labeled as credit note/credit memo/credit invoice, debit note/debit memo, storno, correction, adjustment, refund, reimbursement, or reversing document. Commonly references an original invoice number/date or prior billed item, includes a reason for the adjustment, and shows amounts that apply back to the customer account (often negative line items and negative totals, sometimes shown in parentheses).|
|`other`|`prebuilt-documentFields`|Document whose type cannot be confidently determined from the available OCR text. Common when the page is a fragment, heavily redacted, extremely low-quality OCR, or mixed/irrelevant content where no single structured document type is identifiable.|
